Next Level is designed for two clinician profiles: (1) clinicians who have already transitioned into non-clinical roles — such as Customer Success, Clinical Strategy, Account Management, or Consulting — and want to accelerate their performance and leadership trajectory; and (2) clinicians still in clinical or healthcare organizational roles who want to expand their influence, advocate at the leadership level, or prepare for a pivot into industry.
No. Next Level serves both clinicians who have pivoted and those who are still practicing clinically. If you are active in a healthcare organization and want to lead at a higher level — or are exploring a transition — this program is designed for you.
The core of Next Level is live practice and real feedback — not passive content consumption. That requires a small group where everyone gets airtime, everyone gives feedback, and the facilitator knows your specific professional context. At 12 participants, you get the benefits of a cohort without the anonymity of a large class.
All sessions are recorded and distributed to participants. Because the program is built on live practice and peer interaction, we strongly encourage attendance at all sessions — but we understand real life happens. Recordings will be shared within 48 hours of each session.
Many employers will fund professional development — especially when the value to the team is clearly framed. Next Level is a professional development and leadership program — not a CEU-accredited course. That said, many licensing boards and employers recognize professional development programs like this for leadership, strategic communication, and continuing competency requirements. We recommend checking with your licensing body or HR department. For clinical participants seeking CEU credit, this may qualify under "professional development" categories depending on your state and discipline.
