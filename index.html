No. Next Level serves both clinicians who have pivoted and those still practicing clinically. If you want to lead at a higher level — or are exploring a transition — this program is designed for you.
The core of Next Level is live practice and real feedback — not passive content consumption. At 12 participants, everyone gets airtime, everyone gives feedback, and the facilitator knows your specific professional context.
All sessions are recorded and distributed to participants within 48 hours. Because the program is built on live practice and peer interaction, we strongly encourage attendance — but we understand real life happens.
Many employers will fund professional development — especially when the value is clearly framed. We’ve included letter templates in the Resources section above, one for non-clinical/corporate roles and one for clinical/healthcare org roles.
